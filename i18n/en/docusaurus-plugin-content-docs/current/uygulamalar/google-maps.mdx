## Install and sign in

1. In FrostNav, open **Apps → Server**, select **Google Maps**, and tap **Install**.
2. Follow the [microG](./microg.mdx) guide to grant the required permissions under **Self-Check** and add your Google Account to microG.
3. Open Google Maps, tap the profile icon in the top right, and select the account you added in microG.

## Project to the instrument cluster

1. In FrostNav, go to **Instrument cluster → App → Change** and select **Google Maps**.
2. Tap **Project**.
3. To show navigation cards on the cluster, grant FrostNav **notification access** and enable **turn-by-turn cards** under **Instrument cluster → Google Maps**.

See the [instrument cluster guide](../kullanim/projection.mdx) for details.
