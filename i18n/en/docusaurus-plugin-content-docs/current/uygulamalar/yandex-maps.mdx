## Install and open Navigator mode

1. In FrostNav, open **Apps → Server**, select **Yandex Maps**, and tap **Install**.
2. Open **Yandex Maps** and tap **Navigator** on its home screen.
3. Choose your destination and start the route.

## Project to the instrument cluster

1. In FrostNav, go to **Instrument cluster → App → Change** and select **Yandex Maps**.
2. Tap **Project** and check that projection is active.
3. To show turn instructions and the estimated arrival time on the cluster, enable the **FrostNav accessibility service** and turn on **Yandex → Cluster**.

See the [instrument cluster guide](../kullanim/projection.mdx) for details.
