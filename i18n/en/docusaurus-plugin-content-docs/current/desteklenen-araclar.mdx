FrostNav runs on the multimedia screen of Chery group vehicles. Check whether your car is on the list.

## Supported

| Model | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Jaecoo 7 / Jaecoo 8 | 2025 and later | |
| Omoda 5 Pro | 2024 and later | 145 HP only |
| Tiggo 7 / Tiggo 8 | 2025 and later | 145 HP only. There are two screen types, see below. |

### Tiggo screen types

Tiggo cars come with two screen types:

- **Unified screen:** the instrument cluster and the multimedia screen form one combined panel.
- **Landscape screen:** the multimedia screen is a separate landscape screen in the middle.

The install steps differ slightly by screen type. Find the section for your car on [Install by vehicle](./kurulum/araclar.mdx).

## Partly supported

| Model | Works | Does not work |
|---|---|---|
| Omoda 9 | Install (same ADB method) and map projection | Camera 360 |

## Likely to be supported

- PHEV models
- Chery Arrizo 8

These cars are not verified yet.

## Not supported

- Engines other than 145 HP (e.g. 183 HP)
- Tiggo 8 Pro
- Avangard, 290T and similar models

## Next step

If your car is supported, go to the [install guide](./kurulum/index.mdx). The guide first asks you to find your software version.
Whether you need `adbauth.key` is explained on [Install by vehicle](./kurulum/araclar.mdx).
