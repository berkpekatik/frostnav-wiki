The Wallpaper screen changes the wallpaper on the car's own home screen. Its side-menu name is "Wallpaper" and the screen title is "Static Wallpapers".

:::note

On vehicles with the joined 1920x720 screens, such as the Tiggo 7, the car's home screen cannot show a custom wallpaper. On these vehicles "Wallpaper" is not in the menu.
On Tiggo 8 and Jaecoo vehicles the image is applied straight to the car's home screen.

:::

## Upload an image

1. Tap **Wallpaper** in the side menu.
2. Tap "+ Upload Image (4K / HD)" and pick an image.
3. The image is fitted to the car's screen resolution without distortion and saved in the `SVWallpaper` folder.
4. Select the image and tap "Apply Background".

You can also add images from a computer: copy the file into the car's `/storage/emulated/0/SVWallpaper` folder (you can send it with [Transfer](./web-server.mdx)) and tap "Refresh".

## Your account images

The "My account images" section shows the images you uploaded to your FrostNav account. You can add up to 5 images to your account.

- Add images from the [FrostNav mobile app](./mobile.mdx) or from adbauth.frostnav.com → Wallpapers.
- Tap an image and choose "Download & apply". The car downloads the image, fits it to the screen and applies it.
- The downloaded image stays in the `SVWallpaper` folder, so you can apply it later without internet.

This section needs the car to be linked to your account and online.

## Go back to the factory scene

Tap "Restore Factory Scene". The car's factory wallpaper comes back. Your saved images are not deleted.

## Delete an image

Select the image, tap "Delete" and confirm. Only images in the `SVWallpaper` folder can be deleted. If the deleted image is in use, the car goes back to the factory scene.
