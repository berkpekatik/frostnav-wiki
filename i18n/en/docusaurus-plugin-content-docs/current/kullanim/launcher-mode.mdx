Full-screen home turns FrostNav into a widget home screen. Vehicle info, music, climate, seats and apps sit on one screen. You can use separate layouts for parked and driving.

## Turn it on

1. Open the FrostNav app.
2. Tap "Home screen" in the left menu.
3. Choose "Yes" for "Full-screen home".

The same option is also on the [Settings](./settings.mdx) screen.

## Make it the home screen

1. Choose "Yes" for "Full-screen home".
2. Tap the "Change default home app in system settings" link.
3. Choose FrostNav from the list.
4. The car's Home button now opens the FrostNav home screen.

:::note

Some cars do not show this selection window. In that case the app opens the system settings.

:::

## Turn it off

1. On the home screen's top bar, tap the gear icon. FrostNav settings open.
2. Choose "No" for "Full-screen home".

When it is off, the Home button opens the car's own home screen.

## Edit the home screen

:::note

Editing opens only while the car is parked. If the car is moving, the screen says "Editing is available only while parked".

:::

1. Tap the pen icon on the top bar. Editing opens.
2. Tap the area you want to change. The screen says "Tap an area to change it".
3. Choose a widget from the list. Widgets already on the screen look faded.
4. To empty an area, tap "Remove".
5. When you finish, tap "Done".

On the edit screen you can switch between "Park layout" and "Drive layout". The "Default" button resets the open layout. Your changes are lost.

## Widgets

| Group | Widgets |
|---|---|
| Vehicle | Vehicle, Drive mode, Instant consumption, Trip, Totals, Vehicle health, Air quality, Door lock, Trunk |
| Comfort | Driver seat, Passenger seat, Steering wheel heating, Rear speaker |
| Media and road | Media, Navigation, Phone, Equalizer |
| Shortcuts | App shortcut, Web panel, Automation, Split screen, Mirror to cluster |
| Other | Clock, Photo, Break, Dim screen |

Tapping the "Dim screen" widget turns the screen fully black. This widget writes nothing to the car or the system settings.

If your car does not provide a value, the related widget cannot show it. Use [Data Check](./settings.mdx#veri-kontrolu) to see which widgets receive data.

### Navigation: Home and Work {#ev-is}

The Navigation widget has "Home" and "Work" buttons.

1. If a place is not set, the button says "Tap to set". Tap it.
2. Enter the place as latitude, longitude. Example: `41.0082, 28.9784`. Tap "Save".
3. Once set, tapping the button starts the route full screen in the chosen map app (Google Maps or Yandex).
4. Press and hold the button to change or delete the place.

During guidance the widget shows the next turn, the remaining distance and the arrival time. Give FrostNav notification access for this. "End" closes the guidance.

### Vehicle alerts {#arac-uyarilari}

Alerts from the car's own sensors appear on the home screen. If your car lacks a sensor, that alert does not appear.

| Alert | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| Low tire pressure | The car scene and the alert strip, with the wheel and its psi value. 4x4 cars only. |
| Service soon / Service due | The Vehicle health widget. "Until service" km and days are read from the car's service counter. |
| Low fuel | The Mini dashboard. |
| Poor air quality | The Air quality widget. If the outside air is poor, it says "Recirculation advised". |

### Mini dashboard {#mini-gosterge}

The Mini dashboard puts the clock, range, fuel, a small car view, alerts and the playing music in one panel.
It opens next to navigation in [split screen](./split-screen.mdx) or full screen. While full-screen home is on, a "Mini dashboard" icon also appears in the app list.

## Home screen settings

Tap "Home screen" in the left menu. "Full-screen home" must be on to change these settings.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Theme | Auto follows the car system's day and night mode. Light and Dark are fixed. |
| Drive mode colors | When on, the accent color follows the drive mode. |
| Sport mode (experimental) | When on, a chronograph screen opens while the car is in Sport. It is off by default. |
| Bottom bar | Original: the car's bar stays visible. FrostNav: the home screen's own bar is shown. Hidden: no bar, and widgets use the full height. |
| Climate temperature scale | "Half degree" is right for most cars. Choose "Whole degree" if the climate stops at 23.5 instead of HI. |
| Vehicle screen | Auto, Tiggo 7, Tiggo 8 or Jaecoo. Auto picks from the screen size. Tiggo 7 uses a 12×6 grid, Tiggo 8 a 12×8 grid; Jaecoo is a portrait screen (car on top, four cards below). Each has its own layouts. The change applies the next time the home screen opens. |
| Climate air buttons | When on, face, feet, windshield and recirculation buttons appear next to the fan on the FrostNav bottom bar. When off, the bar shows only temperature and fan. |
| Home button | FrostNav: the car's home button returns to the FrostNav home screen; pressing it there opens the car's own home screen. Car: the home button always opens the car's home screen. |
| Start type | Earliest: the home screen appears as soon as the app is ready at startup. When car has power: it appears once the car's power state is read. |
| Radio command type | The right type is chosen automatically. If AM/FM switching on the media card does not work, try Type 1 or Type 2. |
| Car colour | The color of the car in the middle of the home screen. |
| Separate drive layout | When on, the Drive layout shows while moving and the Park layout shows when stopped. |

The reset buttons on the same screen return the Park and Drive layouts to default.

## Settings on the home screen {#ana-ekran-ayarlari-dislisi}

The gear icon on the home screen's top bar opens a separate settings page:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Favorite key (steering wheel) | A short press of the favorite key on the steering wheel opens Yandex Navi or Yandex Maps. The default is Off. The chosen app must be installed. |
| Phone priority | When on, the car switches to Bluetooth when music starts on the phone. When off, the radio keeps the sound and the phone stops. |
| Long-press voice key opens phone assistant | A long press of the voice key on the steering wheel opens the phone's voice assistant. A short press opens the car's own voice command. |
| Voice assistant button | Shows the voice assistant button on the phone card. When off, the last call gets bigger and up to 5 favorites show. |
| Factory climate panel on change | If set to hide, changing temperature or fan from the bottom bar does not open the car's climate panel. Swiping up on the bar and the physical climate buttons always open it. |
| Vehicle settings / FrostNav settings | Opens the car's own settings or FrostNav's screens. |

## Bluetooth and calls

1. Tap the Bluetooth icon on the top bar. The Bluetooth panel opens.
2. Choose a phone from the paired devices list. Tap "Connect".
3. If you turn on "Auto-connect", the car connects to the last phone when it starts.

When a call starts, the call page opens on the screen. For an incoming call, tap "Answer" or "Decline". During a call you can mute, move the call to the phone, or minimize the page.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| It says "Waiting for car data" | Make sure the car is running. Wait a moment. |
| The home screen does not appear at startup | On the [Settings](./settings.mdx) screen, turn on "Open the app on screen" and "Run in the background". Make FrostNav the home app. |
| Editing does not open | Stop the car. Editing opens only while parked. |
| The climate stops at 23.5 instead of HI | Choose "Whole degree" for "Climate temperature scale". |
| No music information | Give FrostNav notification access. |
| You want the normal view back | Choose "No" for "Full-screen home". |
