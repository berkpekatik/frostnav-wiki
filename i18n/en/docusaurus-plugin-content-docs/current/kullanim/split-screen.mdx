Split Screen opens two apps at the same time. On a landscape screen the apps sit side by side. On a portrait screen one sits above the other. The screen is split into two windows at the ratio you choose.

## Open the screen

1. Open the FrostNav app.
2. In the side menu, touch "Split Screen".
3. The first time, an early access notice appears. Touch "I accept" to continue. The notice is asked once.

Read the status line.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Free-form windows are on. Ready. | Split Screen can start. |
| Free-form windows are off. They will be turned on when you start. | "Split and open" turns free-form windows on itself. |
| This vehicle does not allow free-form windows. | Split Screen cannot start on this car. |

## Open two apps

1. On the "App 1" row, touch "Select app…". Choose an app from the list.
2. Choose a different app for "App 2". The same app cannot be on both rows.
3. Choose "Window ratio".
4. Touch "Split and open".

| Ratio | Result |
|---|---|
| 1/3 · 2/3 | App 1 takes one third of the screen. App 2 takes the rest. |
| 1/2 · 1/2 | Each app takes half of the screen. |
| 2/3 · 1/3 | App 1 takes two thirds of the screen. App 2 takes the rest. |

If it works, the screen says "Split Screen started".

:::note

FrostNav is not the device owner. If an app is already open, it may not take the new size. Close that app first, then touch "Split and open".

:::

## Quick split gesture

If you choose a swipe, that swipe on the vehicle screen opens the saved pair at the chosen ratio.

1. Choose both apps and the ratio first. The gesture opens the saved pair.
2. Under "Quick split gesture", touch one gesture. The rows are 2 fingers, 3 fingers, and 4 fingers. The directions are Up, Down, Left, and Right.
3. The chosen gesture appears on the "Selected" line. The screen tells you how many fingers to use and which way to swipe.
4. Touch the vehicle screen with that many fingers at the same time. Keep the fingers together and swipe in the chosen direction. The vehicle system detects the swipe.

To turn the gesture off, touch "Turn gesture off".

:::note

If another feature already uses the same swipe, Split Screen cannot select it. The screen says "The … feature already uses this swipe, so Split Screen cannot use it." Turn that gesture off on the [map swipe](./projection.mdx) or [sunroof](./sunroof.mdx) screen first.

:::

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Please select both apps first | Fill in both the App 1 row and the App 2 row. |
| Choose two different apps | Do not put the same app on both rows. |
| Free-form windows are not allowed | This car does not support Split Screen. |
| An open app does not change size | Close the open app. Touch "Split and open" again. |
| One of the apps could not be opened | Make sure the app is installed. Choose another app. |
| The gesture does nothing | Make the gesture on the vehicle screen. The finger count and the direction must match the selected gesture. If map swipe or the sunroof uses the same gesture, turn it off. |
