Sound settings apply a five-band equalizer and a loudness boost to the system audio. The boost goes up to 15 dB. A limiter keeps high gain from clipping the peaks and crackling the speakers.

## Open the screen

1. Open the FrostNav app.
2. Touch "Sound settings" in the side menu. On the home panel, touch the "Sound settings" shortcut.

If the device does not support an equalizer, the screen says "Equalizer is not available on this device."

## Turn the equalizer on

- **On:** The curve and the loudness boost apply to the system audio.
- **Off:** The effects turn off. The saved setting stays.

So the setting also stays on when the ignition turns on, keep "Run in the background" On in [Settings](./settings.mdx).

## Loudness

1. On the "Sound & Equalizer Controls" card, move the "Loudness" slider.
2. The value is shown in decibels.

## Presets

In "Presets", touch a name. If you move the sliders by hand, the status becomes "Custom".

| Preset | What it does |
|---|---|
| Flat | Resets the bands. |
| Bass | Brings the bass forward. |
| Vocal | Brings the midrange forward. |
| Treble | Brings the treble forward. |

Touch "Reset to Flat" to make every band flat.

## Bands

On the "5-Band Graphic Equalizer" card, each band has its own slider.

| Band | What it adjusts |
|---|---|
| Sub-Bass | The lowest bass. |
| Mid-Bass | The middle bass. |
| Midrange | Speech and the middle frequencies. |
| Upper Mid | Clarity. |
| Treble | The highest sound. |
