The screen title is "Akıllı Cam Tavan Otomasyonu". It closes the roof when the sun is strong. It opens the roof in cloudy weather or at sunset. The side-menu name is "Sunroof Auto". The labels on this screen stay in Turkish.

:::note

Do not force a roof or a shade the car does not have. It depends on the hardware and the software. It may not work on every model.

:::

## Open the screen

1. Open the FrostNav app.
2. Touch "Sunroof Auto" in the side menu.

So the automation can run when the ignition turns on, keep "Run in the background" On in [Settings](./settings.mdx).

## Live status

"Anlık hava ve ışık durumu" shows the measured light in lux. There are three badges: Cam Tavan, Yağmur, and Araç Hızı. If the sensor is not ready yet, the screen says "Sensör Okunuyor...".

## Automatic sunroof

1. Turn on the "Otomatik Cam Tavan" switch. It manages the roof from the sun, the clouds, and the sunset.
2. In "Ne Yönetilsin?", choose a scope.

| Scope | What it controls |
|---|---|
| Yalnızca Perde | Controls the shade. The glass roof does not open. |
| Tavan + Perde | Controls the glass roof and the shade together. |

## How it should work

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| Akıllı Güneş Koruması | Closes in harsh overhead sun. Stays open in cloud, shade, evening, and night. This is the recommended mode. |
| Sadece Gölgede Aç | Closes at the first sun. Stays open in the shade. |
| Açık Hava Keyfi | Keeps the roof open. Closes only in very harsh sun. |
| Her Sürüşte Daima Aç | Ignores the light. Opens the roof each time the car starts and keeps it open. |

### Sun sensitivity

This section is hidden while "Her Sürüşte Daima Aç" is selected.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Hassas | Closes the roof early. |
| Dengeli | The recommended range. |
| Geniş | Closes the roof late. |

## Safety

| Switch | When it is on |
|---|---|
| Yağmur Emniyeti | Closes the roof when rain is detected. You can still open it by hand. It is on by default. |
| Yüksek Hız Koruması | Closes the roof above 100 km/h. It is on by default. |
| Gece / Karanlıkta Kapat | Closes the roof when it gets dark. It is off by default. While it is off, the roof can stay open at night. The roof then closes only in the sun. |

## Quick swipe control

Choose one swipe for each action. When you make that swipe on the car screen, the roof or the shade moves. Touch the box to try the action.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tavanı Kapat | Closes the glass roof. |
| Tavanı Aç | Opens the glass roof. |
| Perdeyi Kapat | Closes the shade. |
| Perdeyi Aç | Opens the shade. |

1. Touch "Hareketi değiştir" next to the action.
2. Choose the finger count and the direction. The rows are 2 fingers, 3 fingers, and 4 fingers. The directions are Up, Down, Left, and Right.
3. Touch "Hareketi kapat" to turn the gesture off.

One swipe can belong to only one feature. If [split screen](./split-screen.mdx) or [map swipe](./projection.mdx) already uses that swipe, you get a warning. Turn that gesture off first.
