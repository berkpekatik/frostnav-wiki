This guide shows how to use the FrostNav app after the install. If the app is not on your car, use the [install guide](../kurulum/index.mdx) first. For the phone app, see [Mobile app](./mobile.mdx).

## How to move in the app

- The app has a side menu on the left. It has these items: Wi-Fi, Apps, Transfer, Drive Memory, Welcome, Sound settings, Automation, Sunroof Auto, Face Recognition, Camera 360, Split Screen, Instrument display, Vehicle information, Settings, Home screen, and Wallpaper.
- When "Full-screen home" is Yes, the Home button opens FrostNav's widget home screen. See [Full-screen home](./launcher-mode.mdx) for details.
