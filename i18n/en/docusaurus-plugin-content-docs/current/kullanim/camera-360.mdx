Camera 360 shows the four cameras around the car: Front, Rear, Left and Right. You can watch them live,
record to a USB stick or to in-car storage, and see the camera on the side you signal on the instrument cluster.

Surround cameras are available only on the car unit. Not every model has cameras.

:::danger Safety

- Do not look at the camera image while driving. Use Live Preview while the car is stopped.
- Camera 360 does not replace your mirrors, a shoulder check or the car's own parking camera.
- Do not rely on recording always running. The cameras can be busy, the USB stick can fill up or be removed. Copy an important recording somewhere else right away.

:::

:::warning Early access

This feature is in early access. Do not force a function your car does not have.
You use it at your own responsibility.

:::

## Recommended use {#onerilen-kullanim}

| What do you want? | Use | Recommended setting |
|---|---|---|
| Record every drive like a dashcam | [In-Car Recording](#arac-ici-kayit) | "Record automatically with ignition" on. No USB needed; old clips are removed automatically. |
| Record a long trip in more detail and copy it to a computer | [USB Recording](#usb-kayit), with ignition | 960×600. If the screen lags while recording, 640×400. |
| Record a short moment sharply | [USB Recording](#usb-kayit), by hand | HD (1280×800). Touch "Stop Recording" when done. |
| Look around while parking | [Live Preview](#canli-onizleme) | Use it while the car is stopped. |
| See the blind spot when changing lanes | [Side camera on cluster with turn signal](#sinyal-kamerasi) | Cluster projection must be on. |
| Keep the recording of an incident | [Viewer](#izleme-ekrani) | Select the clips and touch "Copy to USB". |

Pick only one of the two automatic recordings. In-car recording and USB auto recording cannot both be on.

If you record while CarPlay or Android Auto is on, keep the resolution low. If the screen still lags, do not record while either is on.

### Does camera recording harm the car? {#araca-etkisi}

No. Recording takes the image from the car's own camera system and writes it to storage:

- No command is sent to the car or the gearbox, the camera software is not changed, and no permanent fault code appears at the service.
- The cameras do not turn on by themselves. They are used only when you start live preview or recording.
- Automatic recording, the turn-signal camera and park photo are off by default. Automatic recording runs only if you turn it on, and only while the ignition is on.
- A fisheye image in recordings is normal. "Dewarp" applies only to the live preview.

## While recording is on {#kayit-acikken}

:::warning Do not close the app while recording

If FrostNav closes suddenly while the cameras or recording are on, the screen can go black or the image may not come through properly.
If that happens, [restart](../bilinen-sorunlar.mdx#yeniden-baslatma) the multimedia unit; the screen returns to normal. This is not a permanent fault recorded in the car. To avoid this:

- **Do not swipe FrostNav away** from the recent apps screen.
- **Stop recording before updating** FrostNav. When you update from inside the app, FrostNav stops recording itself and asks you first.
- To stop recording, use the "Stop" / "Stop Recording" button in the app, or turn off automatic recording.

:::

- Do not remove the USB stick while USB recording runs. Stop recording first. Removing the stick stops recording, and the last clip can be cut short.
- Recording keeps running in the background. The notification says "Camera 360 recording" and a red "Recording in progress" bar appears on the screen.
- Camera 360 loads the processor while recording. If the screen slows down together with map projection, lower the resolution.

## Choosing a USB stick {#usb-bellek}

- The stick must be **FAT32**. See [FAT32](../kurulum/fat32.mdx).
- Prefer a stick made for continuous writing (e.g. "High Endurance" or one sold for dashcams). Ordinary sticks can wear out quickly under constant recording.
- For automatic USB recording to start, the stick must have room for at least 10 minutes of recording.
- Recording is split into 3-minute clips. As the stick fills up, **the oldest clips are deleted**. Copy clips you want to keep somewhere else.

Approximate file sizes (four cameras in one mosaic file):

| Resolution | Per minute | Per hour | On a 32 GB stick |
|---|---|---|---|
| HD (1280×800) | ≈ 60 MB | ≈ 3.6 GB | ≈ 8 hours |
| 960×600 | ≈ 34 MB | ≈ 2 GB | ≈ 15 hours |
| 640×400 | ≈ 15 MB | ≈ 0.9 GB | ≈ 35 hours |

The on-screen estimate line calculates this for your own stick.

## Open the screen

1. Open the FrostNav app.
2. Touch "Camera 360" in the side menu.
3. The first time, an early access notice appears. Read it and touch "I accept" to continue. It is asked only once.
4. The screen says "Choose a mode". There are four cards and the turn-signal camera setting below them.

| Card | What it does |
|---|---|
| Live Preview | Watches the four cameras live. |
| USB Recording | Records the four cameras to a USB stick as MP4. |
| In-Car Recording | Records automatically to in-car storage when the ignition is on. |
| Viewer | Plays clips, copies them to USB or deletes them. |

If a card says "Disabled", the other automatic recording is on. Turn that off first.

## Live Preview {#canli-onizleme}

1. Touch the "Live Preview" card.
2. Touch "Start Preview".
3. The four cameras appear together. Touch a camera to enlarge it. Touch it again to go back.
4. The "Dewarp" switch corrects the fisheye image.
5. "Capture 1 Frame" saves the current frame.
6. When you are done, close the preview with "Stop".

If there is no signal, the screen says "No signal · retrying". If the car's own camera screen is open, the cameras can be busy; wait a moment and try again.

## USB Recording {#usb-kayit}

1. Plug in the stick. Touch the "USB Recording" card.
2. If the stick is not detected, the screen says "No USB storage connected. Plug in a USB drive." Plug it in and touch "Refresh".
3. Under "Recording Settings", choose a "Resolution": HD (1280×800, most detail), 960×600 (balanced) or 640×400 (smallest file).
4. "Start Recording" starts recording. "Stop Recording" ends it.

The four cameras are written into one mosaic MP4. Files are saved to the app folder on the stick as 3-minute clips.

### Automatic USB recording with ignition

1. On the USB Recording screen, turn on "Auto-record with ignition".
2. While the ignition is on and the USB stick is plugged in, the app records on its own at the chosen resolution.
3. As the stick fills, the oldest clips are deleted. When no clip is left to delete, the status becomes "USB is full" and recording stops.

If you stop automatic recording with "Stop Recording", it does not restart for that drive. It starts again at the next ignition.

## In-Car Recording {#arac-ici-kayit}

In-car recording is a dashcam mode that records every drive without a USB stick.

1. Touch the "In-Car Recording" card.
2. Turn on "Record automatically with ignition".
3. Read the "In-car automatic recording" window. Touch "Confirm" to continue or "Cancel" to back out.

Recording starts while the ignition is on and the status becomes "Recording". With the ignition off the status is "Waiting for ignition".
The four cameras are saved into one mosaic file at 960×600, as 3-minute clips.

"Stop for this drive" stops recording for that drive. "Resume" starts it again.

### Storage

- At least 2 GB of free space is always kept on the device.
- Recording space = free space + clips − 2 GB. The bar on the screen shows clips, other files, the 2 GB reserve and the space open for recording.
- When free space nears the 2 GB reserve, the oldest clip is deleted. Deleting stops once there is room. Clips are not deleted by date.
- In-car storage is limited. Copy clips you want to keep to USB with the [Viewer](#izleme-ekrani).

For automatic recording, keep "Run in the background" On in [Settings](./settings.mdx).

## Side camera on cluster with turn signal {#sinyal-kamerasi}

As soon as you signal left or right, the camera on that side appears as a small card on top of the instrument cluster. It helps you see the blind spot when changing lanes.

1. On the Camera 360 main screen, turn on "Side camera on cluster with turn signal".
2. Cluster [projection](./projection.mdx) must be on. Without projection the card does not appear.
3. Optionally use "Flat view" to reduce the fisheye effect of the side camera. This uses a bit more processor.
4. Under "Card position and size", set "Distance from edge", "Vertical position" and "Size". The left and right cards mirror each other. "Reset" restores the defaults.

While you adjust, a preview appears on the cluster; the turn-signal camera does not open during this time. Without a preview the screen says "Turn on cluster projection to see the preview."

:::note

Choose a position and size where the card does not cover the gauges or warning lights. The turn-signal camera is only an aid; keep checking your mirrors.

:::

## Viewer {#izleme-ekrani}

1. Touch the "Viewer" card. The "Open Viewer" button on the In-Car Recording screen opens the same screen.
2. Choose the source: "Car" shows in-car clips, "USB" shows clips on the stick.
3. Touch a clip to play it. "Wide" and "Full screen" enlarge the view. Choose an angle: All, Front, Rear, Left or Right.
4. Touch clips to select them. "Select all" selects all of them, "Clear" removes the selection.
5. "Copy to USB" copies the selected clips to the stick. Without a stick the screen says "Plug in a USB drive to copy".
6. "Delete" deletes the selected clips permanently. This cannot be undone.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The screen went black or shows no image | [Restart](../bilinen-sorunlar.mdx#yeniden-baslatma) the multimedia unit. Next time, do not swipe FrostNav away while recording, and stop recording before updating. |
| The screen lags while recording | Set the resolution to 960×600 or 640×400. If CarPlay or Android Auto is on, close it while recording. Turn off "Flat view" if it is on. Stop recording if you do not need it. |
| No signal | Wait a moment; the app retries. The car's own camera screen may be open. |
| "Camera image is unavailable right now" | The vehicle cameras may be busy. Try again shortly. |
| "Cameras unavailable" | This car has no cameras or they did not start. Restart the car and try again. |
| "Waiting for USB" | Plug in the stick. Make sure it is FAT32. |
| "USB is full" | No old clips are left to delete on the stick. Free up space or use another stick. |
| "Not enough space (2 GB reserve)" | Free up space in the car. Delete unused apps or downloaded files. |
| USB card says "Disabled" | In-car recording is on. Turn it off on the In-Car Recording screen. |
| In-car card says "Disabled" | USB auto recording is on. Turn it off on the USB Recording screen. |
| Recording does not start with ignition | Make sure the switch is on. "Run in the background" must be On. If you stopped it for this drive, touch "Resume". |
| The turn-signal camera does not appear | Cluster projection must be on. The turn-signal camera does not open while the settings screen is open. |
